# Pre-registration records

Before the computations of stages B2, B3 and B4 were run, their success criteria and check tolerances were written down. The paper relies on this ("fixed before the runs").

| file | content | stage / paper section |
|---|---|---|
| `B2_事前登録.md` | original (Japanese), unmodified | B2 / Sec. IV |
| `B3_事前登録.md` | original (Japanese), unmodified | B3 / Sec. V |
| `B4_事前登録.md` | original (Japanese), unmodified | B4 / Sec. VI, Obs. 9 |
| `*_preregistration_en.md` | English translations, **added after the fact** | — |

The Japanese originals are the record. Their SHA-256 hashes are listed in `../MANIFEST.sha256` and checked by `verification/run_all.py`.

The time labels written inside the originals are inaccurate: the author read the clock incorrectly. The translator's notes at the top of each translation give the file modification times recorded in the working folder, and they show that each record was written before the computations it governs produced any result.
